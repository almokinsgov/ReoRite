Reo Rite

/ r-eh-aw / ree + teh /

Māori Pronunciation by Design

Reo Rite is a pronunciation and segmentation framework built to support accurate, respectful synthesis of Te Reo Māori in neural voice systems.

It combines phonotactic-aware logic with open-source tools to help computers and people speak Māori the right way.
