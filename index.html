Reo Rite

/ r-eh-aw / ree + teh /

Māori Pronunciation by Design

Reo Rite is a pronunciation and segmentation framework built to support accurate and respectful synthesis of Te Reo Māori in neural voice systems.

It useds phonotactic-aware logic algorithim to help computers and people speak Māori the right way.
