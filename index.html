ReoRite

Māori Pronunciation by Design

ReoRite is a pronunciation and segmentation framework built to support accurate, respectful synthesis of Te Reo Māori in neural voice systems.

It combines phonotactic-aware logic with open-source tools to help computers – and people – speak Māori the right way.
