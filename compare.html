Reo Rite

Pronunciation comparison demo

This page shows how the Reo Rite correction system improves the pronunciation of Te Reo Māori in text-to-speech (TTS) engines. A range of common and complex Māori words were tested using Amazon Polly's Molly NZ Neural voice. Each word was recorded twice: once with the default pronunciation and once with Reo Rite corrections applied (including segmentation, IPA tuning, and pronunciation adjustments).
